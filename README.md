## Selected case study: Logistics System - LagosLink Deliveries (simulated)

This portfolio uses one case study for all five activities: the database for a simulated parcel delivery company, based on the "Ride-hailing / Logistics System" case study in the brief.

- **Organisation:** a simulated last-mile delivery company in Lagos with three hubs (Ikeja, Yaba and Lekki).
- **Nature of the business:** collects parcels from business and individual customers and delivers them across Lagos using dispatch riders and vans.
- **Users of the database:** customers (book and track deliveries), drivers (accept jobs and update status), hub dispatchers (assign jobs), and the operations manager (performance reports).
- **Major data stored:** customers, drivers, vehicles, hubs, delivery orders, delivery status updates, driver locations and payments.
- **Key operations:** create a delivery request, match it to an available driver, update status from pickup to delivery, track a parcel, take payment, and report on-time performance.
